# CampusLink

CampusLink is a goal-matching and resource-sharing platform for college campuses. It helps students find reliable companions for everyday campus needs and creates a safer structure for interacting with people outside their existing friend circles.

## Core Workflow

1. A student posts a goal with category, description, date/time, and required headcount.
2. Other students discover it on the feed and request to join.
3. The poster approves requests manually or enables auto-accept.
4. Accepted users coordinate through a lobby or private chat.
5. After completion, users confirm the meetup and leave feedback.

## Main Use Cases

- Gym partner matching.
- Cab sharing.
- Sports or activity groups.
- Study plans.
- Notes and resource sharing.
